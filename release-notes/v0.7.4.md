# IncreaseDifficultyMod 0.7.4

- Ice Pistol now holds frozen monsters in place. The native `Enemy.Freeze` only zeroes rigidbody velocity, so monsters that move through their NavMeshAgent kept walking; the agent is now stopped and its path cleared every tick while frozen, and the native freeze timer is kept topped up.
- Link Drone set: while exactly one end is held, the cable length now follows the real gap down and the free end is pulled toward the held end whenever the cable is taut at the documented 0.75 m/s, stopping at 1.25 m. Previously reeling stopped once the held end was more than 0.4 m past the limit, so heavy or snagged loot stayed behind.
- Late Join: the host now closes admission while a scene is being swapped (truck, level, shop, truck) and reopens it once the new scene has finished generating, as L.A.T.E. does; the 2-second safety check still reopens it if generation never reports completion. Returning to the lobby menu is untouched (the game reopens it). Each level change also drops the buffered `UpdateLevelRPC` history, as Rebateman's LateJoin does, so a joiner replays only the latest level.
- Heal Power now multiplies the truck heal by 1.05 per level, compounding (1.05^level: 1.05x at level 1, 1.10x at level 2, 1.63x at the new max level 10; configs `HealBonusPerLevel` and `HealPowerMaxLevel`, replacing `HealPercentPerLevel` and `HealMaxLevel` so old saved values cannot override the new defaults) and applies only to the truck's heal. Heal packs, healing drones and HP regeneration are no longer boosted by it; level modifiers still scale all healing. The Taxman's truck line shows the upgraded number instead of the vanilla one (e.g. `+35` becomes `+37`).
- Gun upgrades (Bullet / Laser / Tranq) now give extra shots instead of a percentage battery saving: +1 shot per level from every battery charge (`GunExtraBulletsPerLevel`, `GunExtraBulletsMaxLevel`, default max level 5), renamed "... Extra Shots". Ice, Poison and Sticky pistols and Spritz are tranq-based and use the Tranq upgrade. Drone Battery Life is unchanged. Owned levels carry over, but a level now means +1 shot.
- Charge Efficiency now uses the same compounding formula and 5% step as Slower Stamina Use: each level multiplies the station's energy drain by 0.95 (95% / 90.25% / 85.7% at levels 1-3, 5% minimum). Config `ChargeReductionPerLevel` replaces `ChargePercentPerLevel`, so an old saved 0.20 cannot override it. Max level is now 10 (config `ChargePowerMaxLevel` replaces `ChargeMaxLevel`; 59.9% energy use at level 10). Slower Stamina Use already worked this way and is unchanged.
- Shop upgrades: the shop floor, the upgrade stand (and its reroll) and quest rewards no longer offer a custom upgrade nobody present can use (every player maxed, or the team level maxed for truck upgrades). Previously the offer was cut off by the team-wide purchase count, so in multiplayer a 5-level upgrade vanished after 5 purchases in total even while some players were still below level 5. Prices already rise with every purchase through the game's own rule (+50% of the base price per purchase, minus 10% per extra player); that is unchanged.
- Three new team quests: **Kill N enemies with guns**, **Extract N valuables** (any condition) and **Extract N valuables worth $X or more each** (X is about a twelfth of the level's quota, at least $1K). They scale with team size, rank and day like the existing ones, are capped by the valuables on the level, and reward the same way. Extraction quests are fed by the same hooks as "Extract N undamaged", counting each valuable once.
- Six more team quests: **Kill N enemies bare-handed** (grabbed or thrown objects, shoves and slams; an enemy that any gun or melee weapon hurt no longer counts), **Stun N enemies**, **Revive N teammates** (multiplayer only; the truck revive and the mod's free auto-revive do not count), **Extract N enemy souls** (the orbs enemies drop, offered when enemies are present), **Close every extraction within M:SS** (10 minutes at rank F, 30 s tighter per rank, never under 5 minutes; shows a countdown and fails at zero) and, only while the Halloween event is active, **A moon is judging: win its approval at an extraction point** (one approved offering per extraction point: 1 at ranks F-D, 2 at C-A, 3 from S, never more than the level has). All use the existing scaling, rewards and HUD.
- New ping visuals: area (surface) pings raise a blue beacon in the world: a rotating rune circle on the floor, a light pillar, a hovering winged spear in a soft halo and rising sparks, with the on-screen arrow kept so the spot can still be found through walls. Monster pings now frame the target with a red targeting reticle that snaps on and rotates, instead of the silhouette outline. Item, valuable, cart and player pings are unchanged.

These changes has been verified in a running game. The new ping visuals have not been checked in a running game yet. Local fixtures (collection, currency, gear, HUD, late join, link sets, network) all pass; they do not exercise monster freezing or cable physics.

## Русский

Ледяной пистолет теперь удерживает замороженных монстров на месте: навигационный агент останавливается на время заморозки. Link Drone: если держать один конец, второй подтягивается к нему (0,75 м/с), пока трос натянут. Late Join: вход закрывается на время смены сцены и снова открывается после генерации. Heal Power: +5% за уровень, только для лечения в грузовике (не аптечки), строка Таксмана показывает улучшенное значение. Улучшения оружия теперь дают +1 выстрел за уровень. Новые командные квесты: убийства из оружия, вынос N ценностей, вынос N дорогих ценностей, убийства голыми руками, оглушение, воскрешение, души врагов, быстрое закрытие эвакуаций и «Луна судит» (только в событии Хэллоуина). Магазин больше не предлагает улучшения, которые никто из игроков уже не может использовать. Новые метки: пинг на поверхность ставит синий маяк (вращающийся рунический круг, столб света, крылатое копьё с ореолом), пинг на монстра — красный прицел с захватом цели. Проверка в игре ещё требуется.
