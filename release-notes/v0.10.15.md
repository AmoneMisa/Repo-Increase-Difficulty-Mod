# IncreaseDifficultyMod 0.10.15 (draft)

A balance pass, elite monsters on deep levels, melee critical hits, new perks and nine new items.

## Balance

- **Difficulty ramp**: monster damage grows +7% per level instead of +10% (x3 now at about 33 completed levels instead of 24). Quota grows +7% per level instead of +10%, and loot value +3% per level (compounding) instead of +2%, so deep quotas no longer outrun the loot.
- **Cheaper expensive perks**: Free Rounds, Free Laser Shot, Free Dart, Unworn Edge, Free Cart Shot and Frugal Drones cost 3 points a level. Free use 4 / 8 / 12%, Free Cart Shot 3 / 6 / 9%, Frugal Drones 5 / 10 / 15%. Deep Regeneration costs 2 points a level and adds +3% a level (up to 45%). Points already spent come back automatically.
- **HP Regeneration** (upgrade) heals twice as fast.
- **Trade-offs**: Sharpshooter now costs 15% of the shots instead of 25% (it used to add only ~1% damage per charge). Ghost Runner: +15% damage taken instead of +30%. Hunter's Eye also gives Monster Threat and Range Finder on your pings.
- **Poison Crossbow**: the poison deals 15% of the monster's max HP (at least 125) over 15 s, so it keeps up with deeper levels; a new bolt keeps half of the poison that was left.
- **Wizard Staff** range 7.5 m (was 5 m). **Flamethrower C.A.R.T.** $50K-$80K (was $75K-$120K) and 30 damage per second (was 24). **Sticky Pistol** glues monsters for 3 s (players still 2 s). **Pulse Ice Pistol**: frozen monsters take 75% firearm damage (was 60%).
- **Surprises**: Reduced Player HP rolls 30-70% (was 10-90%). Skeleton Crew is now Mixed. Iron Lungs is renamed Kevlar Crew (its config switch carries over).
- Values you never changed in the config move to the new defaults once; values you set by hand stay.

## Elite monsters

From 16 completed levels some monsters get one elite trait: 10% of them at 16 levels, +3% per level, at most 40%.

- **Armored**: 30% less damage until it is stunned for real.
- **Regenerating**: after 5 seconds without damage heals 1.5% of max HP a second.
- **Thorned**: reflects 10% of melee damage to the attacker.

Pings with Monster Threat name the trait. Host setting `Monsters/EliteMonsters`.

## Critical hits

- **Critical Strike** (Combat perk, 1 point a level, 5 levels, after Brawler): each melee hit has a 6 / 12 / 18 / 24 / 30% chance to deal double damage. At rank 5 the average hit is x1.3, which is about 1 fewer hit on a monster that took 4-5, 2 fewer on one that took 9 and 3 fewer on one that took 13.
- **Last moon**: a monster attack has a 5% chance to deal double damage to a player (damage-over-time ticks never do). The player sees "MONSTER CRITICAL HIT".

## New perks

- **Toughness** (Survival, 1 point, 3 levels): levitation, constriction, petrification and the twins' grip hold you 10 / 20 / 30% shorter.
- **Hauler** (Utility, 1 point, 3 levels): +5 / 10 / 15% movement speed while you hold loot.
- **Scavenger** (Utility, 2 points, 3 levels): a skill box you open has a 10 / 20 / 30% chance to also drop an Antidote, Adrenaline Shot, Repair Kit or a Smoke, Cryo or Glue grenade.

## New items

- **Smoke Grenade**: a 4.5 m cloud for 12 s; monsters cannot see players inside or through it unless right next to them.
- **Cryo Grenade**: freezes every monster within 4 m for 3 s. Players are unaffected.
- **Glue Grenade**: glues every monster within 4.5 m for 3 s. Players are unaffected.
- **Antidote**: ends burning, bleeding, venom and slowness; no burning, bleeding or venom for 20 s.
- **Adrenaline Shot**: breaks any monster hold, then +30% speed and a full stamina bar for 8 s.
- **Repair Kit**: the most damaged valuable within 3 m gets back half of the value it lost.
- **Boomerang Sickle**: swings like a sword; thrown, it spins, deals 60 damage to every monster in its path and flies back.
- **Armored C.A.R.T.**: loot inside loses 40% less value from knocks and the Mischief Fairy cannot lift it out; 30% heavier.

The new items use tinted vanilla models for now. Switches: `Modules/NewGrenades`, `Modules/Consumables`, `Modules/Sickle`; every player must use the same setting.
