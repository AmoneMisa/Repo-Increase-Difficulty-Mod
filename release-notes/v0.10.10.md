# IncreaseDifficultyMod 0.10.10

Gun batteries that show what they really hold, a new Wide View perk, and better diagnostics for monster damage.

## Gun batteries

- Every mod gun (Poison Crossbow, Pulse Ice Pistol, Sticky Pistol, Net Launcher, Wizard Staff) now spends exactly one battery bar per shot, and the bars on the gun are exactly the shots left. Before, some guns drained a percentage per shot: the bars did not match the real charge, and the gun could stop firing with bars still showing.
- The Poison Crossbow still gets a third fewer shots than the gun it is built on; the Net Launcher keeps its configured number of nets.
- Extra-shot upgrades, skill-tree perks and battery trade-offs now add or remove whole bars, so a shot keeps costing exactly what the gun shows.

## Wide View perk

- New perk on the first page of the skill tree (Recon, Vision branch): your camera sees 5 degrees wider per level, up to +15 at level 3. Sprint, map and other zooms still work.
- Right-click the perk in the skill tree to switch it off or on (also `Skill Tree > WideViewOn` in the config). Only your own screen changes.

## Monster diagnostics

- When one of the mod's monsters is hurt by a hitbox, the host's log now names it: the collider, the monster that owns it, the player behind it and its damage. This is to find what keeps chipping at the Naga Queen at the start of some levels.

Checked by the build and the gear (60) and skill tree (365) test suites, not yet in a live game.

## From 0.10.9

- Courier pets chime once per monster, from only one pet, with a softer sound.
- The Wide cart no longer raises your camera; the flame cart still does.
- A guest who ends up owning the Steam lobby after a connection hiccup hands it back to the host, so "lobby unavailable" no longer sticks for the rest of the session.
