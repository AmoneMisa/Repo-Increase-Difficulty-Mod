# IncreaseDifficultyMod 0.10.13

Monsters that move and sound alive, and new voices for the courier pets.

## Monster animation

- Every attack now reads as wind-up, strike and follow-through. The strike lands exactly when the damage is dealt, swings a little past and settles, and its end flows into the next move instead of snapping.
- The witch cradles a growing flame and winds back before she throws it; her levitation spell sweeps both arms up and thrusts them down at the victim.
- The basilisk rears and hisses before a lunge, draws its neck into an S and snaps shut on the bite.
- The bull scrapes the floor and bellows before a charge, springs into a gallop with its horns forward, recoils after the stop and tosses its head at close range. Its legs splay and wobble when stunned.
- The Naga Queen's sword and four-blade combos come up early so players can read them, then accelerate into the hit.

## Sound

- Monster sounds are rebuilt as voices: a throat-like pulse with breath and moving resonances, instead of plain tone sweeps. Footsteps, wings, scales and impacts have their own texture. Every client hears the same sound.
- Courier pet calls, happy sounds, step and poof are regenerated the same way: warmer, less synthetic, never identical twice in a row.

Checked by the build and the monster test suite (153 checks), not yet in a live game.
