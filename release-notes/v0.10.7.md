# IncreaseDifficultyMod 0.10.7

## Four new courier pets

- The gacha now has sixteen pets. New: **Little Mermaid** (a fish tail instead of legs, long hair with pearls, a starfish and a shell crown), **Skeleton** (a skeleton cat on all fours with aviator goggles and a neckerchief; the delivery pack rides on its back), **Elemental** (a floating stone spirit with a glowing face, core and runes) and **Phoenix** (a fire bird with a flame crest, flame wing feathers and tail plumes).
- Pull chances: Little Mermaid and Skeleton about 4% each, Elemental and Phoenix about 2% each (as rare as the Dragon). The older pets' chances drop slightly to make room.
- Recolour zones, as for every pet (main / second / accessories): Mermaid - scales and tail / skin and fins / hair and gear; Skeleton - bones / neckerchief / goggles and gear; Elemental - stone / energy / runes and gear; Phoenix - feathers / belly / crest and plumes. The Phoenix's bright flame hearts keep their own colour.
- Glowing parts (the Elemental's eyes, core and runes, the Phoenix's flames) are lit from within and follow the colour you pick for their zone.
- The Elemental always hovers a little above the floor. The Skeleton strides on four legs and carries parcels on its back instead of lifting them overhead.
- Each new pet has its own call and three petting sounds.
- Saved pets and looks from earlier versions are unchanged.

## Five new costume sets

Seven parts each (Hat, Body Top, Body Bottom, both arms, both legs), every part in its own slot so it mixes with anything else, and recolourable with the MAIN / SECOND / ACCENT colours. Each set has its own switch in the config (Cosmetics).

- **Pearl Mermaid**: shell crown on long hair, shell top with pearls, scale skirt with rayed tail fins, fin cuffs with gloves, shell boots.
- **Grove Druid**: antlered mossy hood with glowing amber drops and a rune stone, robe under a mantle of separate leaves with a little owl on the shoulder, leaf skirt with a vine belt, tabard and flask, bark bracers, rooted boots.
- **Night Count**: swept pale hair, coat with a high collar, bat-wing cape, jabot and a glowing ruby, striped breeches with a coffin charm, ruffled sleeves, buckled boots.
- **Deep Diver**: brass helmet with a grille and lit portholes, canvas suit with a rope coil and an air tank, breeches with a lantern and an anchor tabard, sealed gloves, weighted boots.
- **Clockwork Toy**: inventor's top hat with goggles, gears and a gauge, uniform with fringed epaulettes and a wind-up key in the back, striped breeches under coat tails with a pocket watch, banded sleeves, piston boots with glowing vents.

The new pets and costumes have been checked in renders and by the art and engine fixtures, not yet in a live game.
