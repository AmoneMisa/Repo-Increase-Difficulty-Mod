# IncreaseDifficultyMod 0.10.12

The map shows the right moon, and the pets' danger warning can be turned off.

## Moon on the map

- With **Level Scaling / ScaleMoon** on, the moon under the level number on the map (Tab) could show the game's own phase (for example moon 5 on level 26 with 7 levels per phase) while the Esc menu and the game itself used the mod's phase (moon 3). The map now shows the same moon as the Esc menu; for guests, that is the host's moon.

## Pet danger warning

- Two new rows on the **MORE** page of the courier menu (C): **DANGER ICON** turns the red "!" over your pets on or off, **DANGER SOUND** turns their alarm chirp on or off. Enter or Left/Right switches them.
- Also in the config as `Courier / DangerIcon` and `Courier / DangerSound`, both on by default. They only change your own screen and sound.

Checked by the build, not yet in a live game.

## From 0.10.11

- Removed the Photon HUD and the Diagnostics switches; Server Info stays.
