# IncreaseDifficultyMod 0.10.14

Smarter pets, team shop upgrades, restart votes for everyone and a rethought Black Market.

## Courier pets

- **Soul Collector** (courier perk, 2 points): the pet carries monster souls lying nearby into a Soul C.A.R.T. within 15 m, holding them out in front in both paws, but only a soul whose whole value still fits under the cart's limit (souls other pets are carrying count too). If monsters knock the pet out on the way, the soul breaks.
- **Pointing out monsters**: when a monster comes near, the pet runs to the doorway toward it, points through it for a few seconds and comes back to you. One pet per monster, again a minute later if it is still around.
- Gacha coins turn up more often: 30% per level instead of 20%.

## Team shop upgrades

Bought in the shop like the truck upgrades; one purchase works for the whole team.

- **Haggling** (3 levels): the shopkeeper robot lowers a price 4 / 5 / 6 times per shop visit instead of 3.
- **Crystal Stock** (3 levels): 6 / 7 / 8 power crystals in the shop at once, never more than the truck needs for a full charge.
- **Spare Gun**, **Spare Melee**, **Spare Drone**: every limited item of that kind can be bought one more time.
- **Upgrade Shelf** (4 levels): two more upgrades in the shop per level (up to +8), on copies of the shop's own upgrade shelf standing against a free wall.
- Every team upgrade has its own packaging and icon. The shop's item info panel (I) shows Russian descriptions and the level the team already owns.
- The shop's extra-upgrades and duplicate-items settings are gone: more upgrades and extra copies now come only from these upgrades.

## Expensive perks

- **Free Rounds**, **Free Laser Shot**, **Free Dart** (7 points a level, 3 levels): a 2% / 4% / 7% chance that a shot of that gun costs no charge.
- **Unworn Edge** (7 points a level, 3 levels): a 2% / 4% / 7% chance that a melee hit costs no durability.
- **Free Cart Shot** (7 points a level, 3 levels): a 2% / 3.5% / 5% chance that a C.A.R.T. cannon or C.A.R.T. laser shot costs no charge.
- **Frugal Drones** (8 points a level, 3 levels): drones use 3% / 5% / 7% less energy.
- **Deep Regeneration** (5 points a level, 5 levels): HP Regeneration fills up to 31% ... 35% of max HP.

## Runs and votes

- **Vote to restart** (Esc menu, any player, on a level): a 20-second vote for [1] the same map, [2] a random map or [3] keep playing. The level restarts only if more than half of the players vote for a restart. One vote a minute; while it is open, keys 1-3 vote instead of switching inventory slots.
- RETURN TO LOBBY now remembers where the run was: a crew that left from the truck comes back to the truck, not to a level that has already started.
- ExtractionMax now counts the start room's extraction point: 5 means 5 quotas, not 6.
- Battery crystals on a retried level can be set up to 30 (MinPerLevel / MaxPerLevel).

## Black Market

- Surprise modifiers are no longer just blocked: `-` makes the chosen one rarer (x0.5, x0.25, never), `+` more common (x1.5, x2, x2.5), one paid step at a time. A step back toward normal is free, and `Space` / `Enter` switches what you bought off and on again for free. Steps that help the crew cost more. Modifiers blocked in an existing run load as "never".
- Level and shop economies are still blocked with `Enter`.

Every player needs this version: several network messages changed.
