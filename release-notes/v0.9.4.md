# IncreaseDifficultyMod 0.9.4

- Added character faces in the Face Top, Face Bottom and Head Bottom slots, fitted round the robot's own eyes: Chel and the Ghost Bride (skin, brows, lips, jaw), and the Rabbid, Musketeer Cat, Bomb Bird, Talking Dog and Swamp Ogre (muzzle, beak, teeth or nose under the eyes instead of on the hood's forehead).
- Chel: one smooth sheet of hair with a blunt fringe, hoop earrings, bandeau and a tied wrap skirt. Ghost Bride: a skeleton left arm and right leg, blue shoulders, an open skirt over the bone leg. Stone Angel: the folded wings sit together at the spine. Assassin: a deep hood with a beak between the eyes.
- Skin, hair, bone, lips and jewellery keep their own colours; cloth, lace and fur still take the slot colour. Hands have no fingers.
- Reworked the assassin, Jack Sparrow and Chel silhouettes; revised the existing bride from the supplied corpse-bride reference and rebuilt the stone angel's folded wings.

- Added four modular outfits: Renaissance Assassin, Pirate Captain, Swamp Ogre and Sun Priestess.
- Added hair for the bride, stone angel and fairy; revised veil flutter, cosmetic material initialization and dark wing colours.
- Attached the teaching skeleton at its upper spine with a visible stand bracket and revised secondary motion.

- The Wide C.A.R.T. is about 17% longer at the default width so the Museum Tray can fit lengthwise.
- Cleanup Crew has a built-in silhouette for pings and map markers.
- Revised Russian wording for quests, markers, skills and modifiers.
